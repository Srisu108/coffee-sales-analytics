# Business Questions

## 1. Overall Business Performance

- What are the total sales, total profit and total units sold?
- How many distinct orders were placed?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- What is the overall profit margin?

## 2. Product Performance

- Which coffee type generates the highest sales?
- Which coffee type generates the highest profit?
- Which coffee type has the highest profit margin?
- Which coffee type has the highest sales volume?
- Which products are the strongest and weakest performers?

## 3. Product Characteristics

### Roast Type

- Which roast type generates the highest sales?
- Which roast type generates the highest profit?
- Which roast type has the highest profit margin?

### Product Size

- Which size generates the highest sales?
- Which size generates the highest profit?
- Which size has the highest unit volume?
- Are profit margins significantly different between sizes?

## 4. Geographic Performance

- Which countries generate the highest sales?
- Which countries generate the highest profit?
- Are there noticeable differences in product performance across countries?

## 5. Time-Based Performance

- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which year generated the highest sales and profit?
- Are there seasonal or monthly patterns?
- Is the latest year a complete reporting period?

## 6. Customer Performance

- How many customers have made purchases?
- What is the average number of orders per customer?
- What is the Average Order Value?
- Which customers contribute the most sales and profit?

## 7. Loyalty Analysis

- How do loyalty-card customers compare with non-loyalty customers?
- Which group generates more sales?
- Which group generates more profit?
- Is there a meaningful difference in average orders per customer?
- Is there a meaningful difference in profit margin?

## 8. Profitability

- Which products and categories contribute most to profit?
- Which products or categories have weak profitability?
- Which product characteristics appear associated with stronger margins?
- Where might the business investigate opportunities for improving profitability?

## 9. Data & Modelling Questions

- What is the grain of the orders table?
- Is `order_id` unique?
- What should be used as the primary key for the order-line table?
- Are customer and product IDs unique in their master tables?
- Are the relationships between orders, customers and products valid?
- How should order counts be calculated when multiple order lines can belong to the same order?
